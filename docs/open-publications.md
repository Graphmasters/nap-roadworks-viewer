# Open publications

These are open DATEX II roadworks and road-closure publications that you can download without an
account. The viewer draws their records on the map. Each entry names the data provider, the licence
and the public dataset page. Read the licence and any attribution requirement before you publish
anything derived from a publication.

Not listed here: publications that need an account, a key, a password or a certificate, such as many
offers on [Mobilithek](https://mobilithek.info) (see [Getting a DATEX II file](get-data.md#naps-with-an-account)),
and publications whose records the viewer cannot draw on the map.

## Nationaal Dataportaal Wegverkeer (NDW), Netherlands

- **Data provider:** Nationaal Dataportaal Wegverkeer (NDW), the Dutch road traffic data portal.
- **Publications:** *Temporary closures* (temporary traffic measures and closures); *Roadworks and
  events planning feed*.
- **Dataset page:** [NDW Open Data Portal](https://opendata.ndw.nu/). Both publications are listed on
  this catalogue page.
- **Licence:** CC0 1.0 (Creative Commons Zero), the default under the NDW open data terms.
- **Attribution:** not required. NDW appreciates it but gives no format.
- **Other restrictions:** the data is provided as it is. NDW gives no guarantee of accuracy,
  completeness, timeliness or availability and accepts no liability. NDW states that it may not own all
  the underlying data, so the terms of a dataset could override CC0. The pages read for these
  publications showed none.

## CITA, Luxembourg

- **Data provider:** Administration des Ponts et Chaussées, Luxembourg. CITA (Contrôle et Information
  du Trafic sur les Autoroutes) publishes the motorway traffic information.
- **Publications:** *PCH : Les chantiers actuels* (current roadworks); *PCH : Les futurs chantiers*
  (future roadworks).
- **Dataset pages:** [PCH : Les chantiers actuels](https://data.public.lu/en/datasets/pch-les-chantiers-actuels/)
  and [PCH : Les futurs chantiers](https://data.public.lu/en/datasets/pch-les-futurs-chantiers/) on
  data.public.lu.
- **Licence:** CC0 1.0 (Creative Commons Zero), as shown on both dataset pages.
- **Attribution:** not required.
- **Other restrictions:** the website cita.lu carries a copyright notice from 2012 that reserves rights.
  It conflicts with the CC0 shown on data.public.lu. The dataset pages say that the update frequency is
  not followed, so the publications may not be current.

## Bison Futé, France

- **Data provider:** Bison Futé, the French road traffic information service.
- **Publication:** *Données évènementielles réseau RRN NC* (events on the national road network).
- **Dataset page:** [Bison Futé open data](https://www.bison-fute.gouv.fr/donnees-sur-la-circulation-du.html),
  in the open-access section.
- **Licence:** [Licence Ouverte 2.0 (Etalab)](https://www.data.gouv.fr/pages/legal/licences/etalab-2.0).
- **Attribution:** name the producing body (the grantor) and the date of the latest update. A link to the
  source can serve. The attribution must not suggest that the public body endorses you or your reuse.
- **Other restrictions:** the grantor gives no warranty and is not liable for reuse; you are responsible
  for your reuse. Reuse must not mislead others about the content, the source or the update date.
  Personal data protection law applies, and French law governs the licence. Some items on the page,
  such as restricted data and data from motorway operators, need credentials and are not open.

## Verkehrsministerium Baden-Württemberg (MobiData BW), Germany

- **Data provider:** Verkehrsministerium Baden-Württemberg (Ministry of Transport Baden-Württemberg),
  published on the state open data portal MobiData BW.
- **Publication:** *Gebündelte Daten Baustelleninformationen Baden-Württemberg* (roadworks information,
  DATEX II).
- **Dataset page:** [MobiData BW, Baustelleninformationen Baden-Württemberg](https://mobidata-bw.de/dataset/baustelleninformationen-baden-wurttemberg)
  (in German).
- **Licence:** [Datenlizenz Deutschland – Namensnennung – Version 2.0](https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/by-2-0)
  (dl-de/by-2-0).
- **Attribution:** give a source note that names the data provider, names the licence (by its name or its
  code dl-de/by-2-0, with a link to the licence text) and refers to the dataset. Flag any changes you make
  to the data. The dataset page gives no wording for the provider's name.
- **Other restrictions:** the dataset page names BEMaS and Autobahn GmbH as the sources of some of its
  resources. The page gives no separate credit wording for them.

Licences and terms can change. Check the dataset page before you reuse a publication.
